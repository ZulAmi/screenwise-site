Screenwise Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Screenwise works entirely on your iPhone. Your screenshots, and everything Screenwise reads from them, never leave it.

No cloud AI

Many screenshot apps send your screenshots to AI services in the cloud to be read. Screenwise never does. It sorts with clear rules and Apple's text recognition, both built into your iPhone, and on iPhones with Apple Intelligence it can also ask Apple's on-device model, which runs on the iPhone itself. No screenshot, and nothing read from one, is ever uploaded.

What Screenwise reads

With your permission, Screenwise reads the screenshots and screen recordings in your Photos library using Apple's on-device text recognition and image labels. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model helps sort screenshots the rules are unsure about. None of this uses the internet.

What Screenwise keeps

A summary of each screenshot (its type, dates, and its text with card numbers, codes, passwords and similar details masked) is saved on your iPhone so the app opens instantly. It isn't included in backups and is deleted when you delete the app. Images are never copied, except into the Vault if you move them there.

Folders

Folders you create in Screenwise are albums in your Photos library, inside a folder called Screenwise. They stay in Photos, and in iCloud Photos if you use it, like any album you make yourself.

The Vault

Screenshots you move to the Vault are encrypted with a key that stays in your iPhone's Keychain and needs Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. They aren't in iCloud or backups.

What Screenwise sends

Nothing. Screenwise has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data. It only goes online when you open a link or a maps or calendar app yourself, and Photos may download a screenshot you keep in iCloud.

Sharing

When you share through Screenwise, Share Guard checks the screenshot on your iPhone first and removes location and camera details from the shared copy. What you share, and with whom, is between you and the app you share to.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Screenwise only learns whether Pro is unlocked.

Reminders

If you turn on expiry reminders, they're scheduled on your iPhone and say only how many screenshots expired, never what's in them.

Children

Screenwise collects no data from anyone, including children.
