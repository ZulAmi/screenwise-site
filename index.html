Screenwise can't see my screenshots

Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Screenwise and choose Full Access. Screenwise reads them on your iPhone and uploads nothing.

Some screenshots weren't read

Screenshots kept only in iCloud are downloaded by Photos first. Screenwise reads them automatically the next time you're on Wi-Fi.

Is deleting permanent?

No. Screenshots you delete through Screenwise go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where they stay for 30 days. iOS asks you to confirm every delete.

Where are my folders?

Each folder is an album in Photos, inside a folder called Screenwise, so you can see them in the Photos app too. Deleting a folder removes the album, not the screenshots in it.

Restoring Screenwise Pro

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, open Settings › Get Screenwise Pro and tap Restore purchases. Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group.

Cancelling a subscription

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Screenwise. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

The Vault

Vault screenshots are encrypted on this iPhone only and aren't in iCloud or backups. Before deleting the app or erasing your iPhone, open each one and choose Restore to Photos, or they'll be lost.
